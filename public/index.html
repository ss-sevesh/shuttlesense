Before your first review

Start witha clear court.

A little setup makes the evidence easier to review. All video analysis and report inference run locally once the models are installed.

  1. 01
    Keep the court in view.

    Use a fixed landscape recording with the near player, court corners and shuttle visible.

  2. 02
    Mark, then review.

    Calibrate the singles court. Check rally boundaries and mark Lost, Won or Unknown yourself.

  3. 03
    Keep the limits in mind.

    Tracking, posture and image gaps are estimates. They cannot confirm first ground touch or the cause of a loss.
